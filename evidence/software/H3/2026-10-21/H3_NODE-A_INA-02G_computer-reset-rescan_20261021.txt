Interpretation
--------------
- state=0x20, CR1=0x0001, SR1=0 and SR2=0 show an enabled, ready and non-busy I2C1 master before probing.
- GPIOB IDR 0x0300 shows PB8/SCL and PB9/SDA were both high before the transaction.
- The expected 0x40 address returned status=1 twice, including after a controlled I2C reinitialization.
- The complete 7-bit scan returned ack_count=0; therefore no alternative responding address was found.
- Manufacturer ID and Die ID reads were not valid because no slave address acknowledged.
- hal_err=0x00000000 on the INA path shows no persistent HAL/I2C controller error after the clean no-ACK result.

Boundary and verdict
--------------------
This computer-only retest independently reproduces the 2026-10-20 result without relying on oscilloscope interpretation. Combined with the prior module-header waveform and passive address-network evidence, INA260-01 remains FAIL / HARDWARE HOLD for a module-level no-response condition. This evidence does not distinguish IC fine-pitch soldering, an internal PCB interconnect fault, or a defective/nonconforming IC. It does exclude treating the earlier visual waveform interpretation as the sole basis for the verdict.

The later periodic CAN STAT lines may show CAN transmit errors because Node B/CAN participation was outside this isolated INA test. Those CAN fields are not INA260 I2C errors and are not used in this verdict.
