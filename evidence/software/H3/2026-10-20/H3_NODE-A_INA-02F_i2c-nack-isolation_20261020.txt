H3 Node A / INA260-01 I2C NACK isolation record
Date: 2026-10-20 (Asia/Shanghai)
Record type: normalized execution record; not a verbatim UART transcript

1. Scope and safety boundary

- Target: Node A, frozen ST-Link SN 066BFF575151676667043206, COM7.
- I2C1: PB8/D15=SCL, PB9/D14=SDA, 100 kHz.
- INA260-01 low-voltage connections only: Vcc=3V3, GND, SCL, SDA.
- Node B and fan 12 V remained off during the identity diagnostics.
- ALERT, VBus, Vin+, Vin-, the green power terminal, fan and heater were not
  added to this test.
- The diagnostic path used HAL_I2C_IsDeviceReady and HAL_I2C_Mem_Read only;
  no INA260 register write path was introduced.

2. Preceding passive and powered observations

- Isolated passive check: Vcc-SCL=10.056 kOhm and Vcc-SDA=10.028 kOhm,
  consistent with the two onboard approximately 10 kOhm pull-ups.
- Powered static check at the module header: Vcc=3.230 V, SCL=3.223 V,
  SDA=3.224 V; no USB disconnect/reconnect was reported.
- The MCU-side four-wire continuity and short-circuit admission checks passed.

3. Read-only scan and master-state result

- Before the expected-address probe the recorded master state was clean:
  state=0x20, hal_err=0x00000000, CR1=0x0001, SR1=0x0000,
  SR2=0x0000, GPIOB IDR PB8/PB9=0x0300.
- The directed 0x40 readiness probe returned status=1 (HAL_ERROR) with
  hal_err=0x00000000. A controlled I2C reinitialization left the peripheral
  clean, and the repeated 0x40 probe produced the same no-ACK result.
- The subsequent 7-bit scan found ack_count=0; therefore no alternate address
  responded and Manufacturer ID / Die ID could not be read.
- The zero HAL error after a completed readiness attempt is retained as
  observed HAL behavior; the bus waveform is used below to distinguish a
  clean slave NACK from an absent master transaction.

4. Scope-capture progression

- Early single-shot captures at the instrument timestamps 19:48 and 20:01
  captured reset/communication activity rather than a uniquely isolated
  INA260 address probe. They are retained as negative evidence and are not
  used to claim ACK or NACK.
- The firmware was then changed so one B1 press issues one 0x40 readiness
  address cycle before any UART message. The resulting diagnostic artifact was:
    freertos.c SHA-256:
      2E1ABED5122D3A70366ABA143BA6FD029B0EB0EFB8B2968F4DE225101E274B97
    node_a.elf size:
      1115360 bytes
    node_a.elf SHA-256:
      C77C8203619DAA7820375E1BA6C57016345D197AAB5E9ED7F957F44ED17B0888
- At 20:05 instrument time, the B1 capture at the intermediate breadboard
  node showed an approximately 100 kHz SCL address cycle and no slave ACK.
- Because that point did not prove arrival at the module itself, the probes
  were moved directly onto the soldered SCL/SDA header pins.
- At 20:13 instrument time, the module-header capture reproduced the same
  approximately 100 kHz address cycle and no slave ACK. This excludes the
  Node-A-to-breadboard and breadboard-to-module signal path as the reason for
  the missing acknowledgement within this test setup.

5. Address-network closeout

With all involved power removed, the two unbridged A0/A1 address jumpers were
measured to the module GND header:

- A0 small left pad to GND: 4.607 kOhm
- A0 large right pad to GND: 9.988 kOhm
- A1 small left pad to GND: 4.609 kOhm
- A1 large right pad to GND: 10.044 kOhm

The equal approximately 4.61 kOhm readings identify the common Vcc-side pads
in this in-circuit resistance measurement. The large A0/A1 signal-side pads
each retain the expected approximately 10 kOhm pull-down to GND, and neither
jumper is visibly bridged. The default 0x40 address network is therefore
present; the full no-ACK scan also rules out a simple alternate-address case.

6. Decision

- PASS: Node A identity, PB8/PB9 configuration, 100 kHz master timing,
  low-voltage wiring continuity, waveform arrival at the module header, and
  the A0/A1 default-address network.
- FAIL/HARDWARE HOLD: INA260-01 produces no ACK at 0x40 or any other scanned
  7-bit address, so register identity and measurement functions cannot start.
- The evidence localizes the fault to the INA260-01 module level. It does not
  non-destructively distinguish a factory IC solder/trace defect, missing IC
  supply at the package, a damaged/counterfeit IC, or another internal module
  assembly fault.
- Do not authorize 12 V, Vin+/Vin-, VBus, load current, or high-side testing
  with INA260-01. Replace or separately repair the module, then repeat the
  same four-wire admission, powered-static, 0x40 ACK, Manufacturer ID and Die
  ID sequence before proceeding.
